# Demo video script

Target length: **3:30**.
Goal: a viewer who has never heard of BookBrain OS understands what it
does, sees it work end-to-end, and wants to buy it.

The script below is a beat sheet, not a literal teleprompter. Times are
approximate.

---

## Beat 0 — Cold open (0:00 – 0:08)

Hard cut. Black screen.

> *(text on screen)*
> "From one idea, to a complete book package."
> *fade in product wordmark: **BookBrain OS***

## Beat 1 — The problem (0:08 – 0:25)

Talking head, voice-over.

> "Writing a book with AI today still means juggling three things —
> ChatGPT for outlines, Claude for drafts, Gemini for research — and
> *you* are the one stitching them together. Then you still have to
> handle the cover, the description, the keywords, the categories, and
> seven slots in KDP."

Visual: split screen of three browser tabs (ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini),
plus a Photoshop window, plus a KDP form. Cluttered.

## Beat 2 — The pitch (0:25 – 0:40)

> "BookBrain OS does all of it from one screen. You enter one idea. It
> runs ten specialized AI steps — research, structure, drafting,
> editing, fact-checking, KDP packaging, cover — and gives you a print-
> ready publishing package."

Cut to dashboard at `/`. Show the New Book button.

## Beat 3 — Onboarding in 30s (0:40 – 1:00)

Speed-up sequence with subtle transitions.

> "Setup takes one wizard. Paste three API keys, click test, paste your
> license, done."

Show wizard steps 2 → 3 → 4 → 6 in 6–8 seconds total. Green dots on the
test step. Confetti or subtle success cue at the end.

## Beat 4 — Create the book (1:00 – 1:20)

Click **New book**. Fill the form live.

> "I'm writing a short business book on three-AI workflows. Working
> title, idea, language, target market, target length — 22,000 words.
> The form even shows the cost estimate before I commit."

Hover the cost estimate ($1.80 or so).

> "And it warns me if my idea looks too close to a book I've already
> written."

Briefly show the duplicate warning (set up in advance with a similar
project). Click **Create project**.

## Beat 5 — Pipeline (1:20 – 2:10)

Land on the project page. Click **Run all**.

> "Each step is a different AI doing what it's best at."

As each step turns green, briefly tab through:
- Research → "Gemini does the market and reader analysis."
- Concept → "James — that's the OpenAI agent — gives a go/no-go."
- Structure → switch to Manuscript tab, show chapter list "Claude lays
  the spine of the book."
- Drafting → "and writes every chapter."
- Editorial / Fact Check → "Independent passes for tone and accuracy."
- Revision → "Claude rewrites with the notes."
- KDP → switch to KDP tab "James packages the title, description,
  seven keywords, A+ content, even the cover prompt."

This whole beat plays at 1.5–2× speed with a steady on-screen counter
showing "$1.42 spent so far."

## Beat 6 — Cover Studio (2:10 – 2:55)

Switch to Cover Studio tab.

> "And now the cover. You can upload your own image — or just have the
> system generate one."

Click **AI generate**. While it runs:

> "It uses the cover prompt the KDP step already wrote. No prompting
> from me."

Image appears. Drop it in. Show the title text auto-filled. Adjust font
size with a slider live.

> "Title, subtitle, author — drag and tweak. Click render."

Show the ebook preview. Then switch to **Paperback**. Type a page count.

> "For paperback, you put in your real page count and trim size. The
> spine width is calculated using KDP's actual formulas. Bleed, safe
> zone, barcode reserve — all here."

Click **Preview with guides**. Show the wraparound with bleed/safe/spine
overlays.

Click **Save PDF + PNG**.

## Beat 7 — Export and ship (2:55 – 3:10)

Click **Download ZIP** in the project header.

> "One ZIP. Manuscript as Word *and* Markdown. KDP description, A+
> content, cover prompt, research report, editorial notes. Cover PDF
> and PNG. Done."

Cut to the customer's KDP dashboard (mocked or anonymized) with the
manuscript and cover dragged in.

## Beat 8 — The compounding moment (3:10 – 3:25)

Cut back to the dashboard. Open `/knowledge`.

> "Here's the part nobody else does. Every finished book becomes
> reusable knowledge — concepts, style DNA, series rules, lessons.
> Your *next* book inherits all of it. Book five sounds like the same
> author as book one. The system gets stronger every time you ship."

Search the knowledge base for a concept and show hits with similarity
scores.

## Beat 9 — Buy (3:25 – 3:30)

Cut to `/pricing`. Hover the buy button.

> "Self-hosted, your machine, your keys, your books. Lifetime license.
> Twenty-nine eight hundred for the first twenty buyers. Link in the
> description."

End card with URL + license badge.

---

## Production notes

- Record at 1920×1080. Browser zoom 110%. Hide bookmarks bar.
- Pre-generate one project and one cover in advance so any "long step"
  cut in the recording still feels live (you'll splice).
- Keep cursor moves slow and deliberate. No fast scrolling.
- Use one accent color for callouts. The product is dark — orange or
  cyan callouts read best.
- Background music: low, instrumental, no lyrics. Drop the music to
  silence at beat 8 (the knowledge moment) for emphasis.
- Caption everything. A surprising number of buyers watch muted.
