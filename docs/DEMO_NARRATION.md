## Recording notes

- Pace: deliberate. Avg 3–6 syllables per second.
- Pause for at least one full second between every numbered cue.
- The four single-line beats at 0:45 / 0:55 / 1:05 / 1:15 are intentional
  — keep them clipped. Rhythm carries the pitch.
- For the silence between 2:10 and 2:25, hold the camera/screen still on
  the dashboard — the pause sells the next reveal.
